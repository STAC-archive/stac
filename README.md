# STAC – Scales and Tasks for Art and Creativity Assessment

**Website: [stac-archive.github.io/stac](https://stac-archive.github.io/stac/)**

STAC is a free archive of self-report scales and lab tasks for research on creativity, aesthetics, and the arts. Researchers may use all tools for academic and non-commercial purposes without asking for permission.

## How this repository is organized

Each measure has its own folder with all its files (language versions, computerized versions such as Qualtrics or MediaLab files, norms, and more) and a `README.md` with information on administration, scoring, and citation.

- `Art and Aesthetics/`
- `Creativity/`
  - `Self-Report Scales/`
  - `Tasks/` (including `Methods & Scoring/`)
- `Personality and Individual Differences/`

The easiest way to search and download measures is the [website](https://stac-archive.github.io/stac/).

## Contribute a measure

We welcome new scales and tasks, translations, and e-copy versions. See the [Contribute page](https://stac-archive.github.io/stac/#contribute) and use [`README_Template.md`](README_Template.md) for your README.

## How to cite STAC

Kenett, Y. N., Silvia, P. J., & Benedek, M. (2026). Scales and Tasks for Art and Creativity Assessment (STAC). *Psychological Test Adaptation and Development, 7*, 239–244. https://doi.org/10.1027/2698-1866/a000140
